I'm Piyush Baniya, a developer from Nepal 🇳🇵 who enjoys turning ideas into polished, useful products.

My current focus is Flutter development, building cross-platform mobile applications with a strong emphasis on performance, clean architecture, privacy, and smooth user experiences.

I've explored multiple areas of development over the years, including Kotlin/Android, React, Node.js, Python, Django, and the MERN stack. These experiences help me approach mobile development from both a frontend and full-stack perspective.

I'm especially interested in building products from the ground up — from the initial idea and architecture to implementation, UI/UX, testing, and Play Store release.

When I'm not coding, I create gaming and tech content on YouTube and experiment with new ideas.

🚀 What I'm Building <br>
🎵 VoraTube

A modern local music player for Android built with Flutter.

VoraTube focuses on playing music that is already stored on the user's device, with a smooth, modern dark/AMOLED interface and features designed around a fast and enjoyable listening experience.

Tech: Flutter • Dart • Riverpod • Drift • just_audio • audio_service
